Field Kit · design board, revision 2

Pushed further, and the call moves to the bar

Two things happen in this revision. The reference's craft is pushed harder: bigger radii, a signature hero surface, spec tiles, and the display type carrying more weight. And the bottom chrome is resolved the other way — Call 999 becomes a permanent beacon inside the tab bar, so the red dock above it is gone and the emergency action never moves.

Two of the four open questions, answered

The voice of the type — answered
The design file said everything was the system font and that a condensed grotesque would suit a field instrument. Overpass, with IBM Plex Mono for measured values, is now bundled and applied — the same two-family split the file proposes: words in one face, numbers in the other.
How much bottom chrome is too much — answered
The file left this open because a red fifth tab "demotes the emergency action to a peer of More". The objection is to a flat tab, not to the bar. This board puts the call in the bar as a wider, lit beacon, heavier than any tab, identical on every screen. One red thing, one place, about 45px of thumb zone back.

Where the emergency action goes, and why this is a real trade

 What it buysWhat it costs
Dock above the bar
shipped today		 A full-width red button, unmissable, in the strongest part of the thumb zone. ~106px of chrome, and the red action vanishes on Act, so its position is not constant.
Beacon in the bar
this board		 One bar. The call is in the same place on every screen, including Act, so it never moves and never disappears. ~45px returned to the content and the thumb zone. Smaller than a full-width button, and it takes a slot beside the tabs — exactly the demotion the design file worried about, answered by making it heavier rather than flat.

The honest weak point: on Act the emergency action is no longer the largest thing on the screen. If that is the wrong trade for the one screen where it matters most, the alternative is to keep Act's body button and let the beacon appear everywhere except Act — which restores the old inconsistency but keeps the big button where it counts. That is a one-line change to react to.

Still open from the design file

The signal colour
Still the in-gamut amber, as close to fluorescent as sRGB allows. The open question — is fluorescent right, or does it read as hazard tape? — is unchanged by this revision and wants a look on a real screen in daylight.
Dark-first, honestly
Unchanged: dark is the default and light is the variant, which puts the light theme at risk of getting less attention — and daytime outdoors is arguably the common case. Worth a daylight check rather than an assumption.

How to read this. Set in the real bundled subsets of Overpass and IBM Plex Mono, from the tokens in src/constants/theme.ts and the roles in src/constants/type.ts. The screen copy is the app's own, verbatim; the beacon, the hero surface and the tiles are the proposed direction, not shipped work. What this is not: a finished layout — spacing is not re-tuned per screen, and the beacon's size, the Act trade above, and the two open questions are all things to react to. The reference board (iAid) informed the craft only; the palette, the register, and the refusal of gamification are Field Kit's own.
