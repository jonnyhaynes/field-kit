Field Kit · design direction 2 of 3

Beacon

A piece of hi-vis equipment. Dark by default, because the moment you need this you may be in a tent, a car at night, or a hillside after the light has gone. Big type, warm black, one hi-vis amber for action, and rescue red that never leaves the bottom of the screen.

The structural bet is the dock. Call 999 is pinned to the bottom of every screen in the app, on the same principle as the emergency exit sign: you should never have to navigate to it, and you should never have to remember where it was. Everything else is a full-bleed command screen built to be read at arm’s length, one-handed.

The bet
Loud and legible beats calm. Like hi-vis, you wear it precisely because it’s not subtle.
Structure
Command screens, not pages. A persistent red dock keeps the emergency action on every screen.
Colour discipline
Hi-vis amber for action, red for emergency, and nothing else coloured.

Depth is still not a mode. The dock is identical for everyone; the Responder depth adds Record and Send behind it, and the first two actions never move. The difference from Direction 1 is that the emergency call is not a screen you return to — it is furniture.

What it gains, what it costs

Gains

  • The emergency action is never lost. No matter how deep someone navigates, Call 999 is one tap at the bottom of the screen.
  • It survives the arm’s-length case — phone on the ground, in the dark, one hand busy. Distance and pace are readable from further away than a body-text screen allows.
  • The night case is the default, not an afterthought, and the amber holds its contrast on both themes.

Costs

  • The dock invites a mis-tap. A red call button always under the thumb needs the confirmation sheet the plan already specifies, or one brush dials 999.
  • It spends a lot of screen on actions. Reference content, which is most of the app, gets less room than it does today.
  • Bigger, louder, more colour. That is the right trade at 2am and arguably the wrong one on a calm weekday, so it is a decision about the worst moment, not the average one.

How to read this file. Dark is the default; add or remove dark mode in your OS to flip the pages, and the second Act frame is forced light so both can be compared. Frames are 320 × 704. The clinical strings are the real cited record or an honest content slot — never invented.
